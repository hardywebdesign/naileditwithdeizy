# Updating your website

You can change your colors, photos and words yourself, any time, from your phone or
computer. Changes show on your site about a minute after you save.

## Signing in
1. Go to **naileditwithdeizy-yy9p.vercel.app/admin**. Bookmark it, or add it to your
   phone's home screen, so it's easy to find.
2. Tap **Log in with Keystatic Cloud** and sign in with your email
   (deizaraejean@gmail.com). The first time, accept the invite email from Keystatic.

## Change your colors for the season
1. Open **Theme and colors**.
2. Pick a look under **Color theme**: Fall, Spooky season, Winter, Valentine's, Spring,
   Summer or Classic. Or pick **Custom colors** and type your own.
3. Update the **Announcement bar** message, like "Holiday sets are here!"
4. Click **Save**.

Don't worry about clashing colors. The site adjusts text automatically so it's always readable.
The little drawings around the site change with the theme too: leaves for fall, bats for
spooky season, snowflakes for winter, hearts for Valentine's, flowers for spring and suns
for summer.

## Change the big photos on the home page
1. Open **Home page**.
2. Under **Top photos**, click a photo to replace it, or **Add** a new one (up to 3).
   The first one shows biggest.
3. Write a short description of each photo (it helps people using screen readers).
4. Click **Save**.

## Add a new set (or remove one)
Your Gallery is your shop. Every set you add shows on the Gallery page with a
**Request this set** button that takes customers to book a free consultation with you.

1. Open **Gallery (your sets)**.
2. Click **Add**, upload the photo, give the set a name and pick its section.
3. Add a short **Description** and a **Price** (like "$25") if you want them to show.
   Both are optional.
4. Tick **Feature on the home page** to show it under "Fresh off the table."
5. Drag sets to reorder them. Use the trash can to remove one.
6. Click **Save**.

## Other things you can change
- **Reviews:** add a new review with the person's first name.
- **FAQ, Policies, About, Services, Sizing & care:** edit any text.
- **Business info and links:** email, phone, TikTok, Facebook, your Calendly booking
  link and tip link.

## How orders work
Customers tap **Request this set** (or **Request a custom set**) and book a consultation
on your Calendly. The set they picked is filled in on the booking form. After the call,
send them a payment link from Square. Questions come to your email through the contact
form.
